Customizing

Settings › Raccourcis (shortcuts) lists every action. Click the pencil, then press the key combination you want.

  • A combination already in use is flagged: reassigning it removes the shortcut from the other action.
  • A key without Ctrl, Alt or ⌘ is refused (except F1 to F12): it would no longer reach the terminal.
  • Copy and paste are fixed.
  • The undo arrow restores an action’s default shortcut; Tout réinitialiser (reset all) restores them all.

Quake mode

Settings › Mode Quake: the window docks at the top of the screen (height from 20 to 100 %) and the global hotkey shows or hides it, even when another app is in front.
